Root basics

What does root mean?

Root gives you superuser access to your Android system It lets you change settings and files that are normally locked

Is rooting legal?

In most countries rooting your own phone is legal Check the rules where you live

Is rooting safe?

It is safe if you follow the right steps for your exact model Mistakes can brick the phone so back up first and use the correct files

Will I lose my data?

Unlocking the bootloader erases your phone Back up photos chats and files before you start

Can I undo root?

Usually yes You can flash the stock boot image or firmware to remove root Samsung's Knox trip cannot be reversed

Does root void my warranty?

Often yes Unlocking the bootloader can void it and the maker may refuse repairs

Apps and updates

Will banking apps still work?

Many banking apps detect root Some can be made to work with modules but there is no guarantee

Will I still get updates?

Official updates may fail or remove root You can usually patch the new update and root again

Why do some apps say my phone is rooted?

Apps check for superuser files and the bootloader state Newer apps also use Google's Play Integrity check

What is Magisk?

Magisk is a popular tool that roots your phone without changing the system partition It also supports modules

Bootloader and models

What is a bootloader?

It is the first program that starts your phone A locked bootloader only loads software from the maker

Can every phone be rooted?

No It depends on whether the bootloader can be unlocked for your exact model and region

Why can't I unlock my Xiaomi phone?

Xiaomi needs a linked Mi account the Mi Unlock tool and a waiting period Some models and regions are restricted

Can I root a Samsung phone?

Yes on many models using the OEM unlock toggle It trips Knox permanently and some carrier models cannot unlock

Can I root an iPhone?

No iPhones run iOS not Android This site covers Android only
